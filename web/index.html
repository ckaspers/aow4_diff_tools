AoW4 mod check · Age of Wonders 4

Source and desktop version

See what your mod changes, and what the game changed under it

Compares every resource your mod overrides with the game as it is installed now, field by field, using the names the ResourceED editor shows. Pick two folders and check: the report opens on this page.

1Game folder

The folder that holds AOW4.exe. In Steam: right-click Age of Wonders 4 › Manage › Browse local files. Usually C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Age of Wonders 4

2Mod folder

Your mod's folder, the one with its .acp file. Usually %APPDATA%\Paradox Interactive\Age of Wonders 4\Mods\<your mod> (type %APPDATA% in the dialog's address bar).

3Check

Takes about half a minute; the first run on a new game build a little longer. Your browser asks whether to "upload" each folder: that is its wording for letting a page read a folder. Nothing is sent anywhere.

What the report shows

OverridesEvery field where your copy differs from the live resource: your own edits and whatever the game changed since you made the copy, side by side.
Tooltip keysPlaceholders nothing fills, and leftover keys that Ok in the Localized String Dialog would drop.
Possibly old contentLinks to resources that no longer exist, and entries only your copy has: your additions, or content the game has since removed.
Deleted by the modResources your mod marks deleted that are still in the game.
No targetOverrides whose resource is gone from the game, with where it moved or under which id it was re-created.
Repeated differencesThe same difference across many resources: a pass you made on purpose, or a game change your copies undo.
Layout-onlyFields at their default on both sides but saved differently. No effect in game.

Stays on your PC

The check runs in your browser. Your game and mod files are read where they are and never leave your computer.

Browsers

Any current desktop browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox. Pick the folders again on each visit; the page remembers nothing about them, apart from a scan of game data in this browser to speed up the next check.

After a game update

Field names are read from the editor. Until they catch up with a new game build, a note says so and new fields show as field_N.
